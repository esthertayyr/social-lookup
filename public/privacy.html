Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Information We Collect

SocialTrace does not require accounts or registration. We do not permanently store search queries. Searches are cached temporarily (up to 24 hours) to improve performance.

How We Use Information

Search queries are used solely to check publicly available social media profiles. We do not sell, share, or distribute search data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings.

Cookies

We use cookies only for advertising purposes through Google AdSense. No tracking cookies are set by SocialTrace itself.

Data Sources

All information displayed comes from publicly available social media profiles. We do not access private accounts or protected information.
