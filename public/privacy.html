Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Information We Collect

SocialTrace does not require user accounts or registration. We do not store your searches on our servers permanently. Searches are cached temporarily (up to 24 hours) to improve performance.

How We Use Information

Search queries are used solely to check publicly available social media profiles. We do not sell, share, or distribute user search data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Cookies

We use cookies only for advertising purposes through Google AdSense. No tracking cookies are set by SocialTrace itself.

Data Sources

All information displayed in search results comes from publicly available social media profiles. We do not access private accounts or protected information.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@socialtrace.com.
